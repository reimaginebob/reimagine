# What's Changed

Reimagine is in active beta, and things get renamed as the product gets clearer. If you used it a few months ago and came back to find a feature you remember, this chapter is where you look it up.

It has two halves, and they work differently on purpose.

## Former names, and what they are now

**This table is permanent. Do not prune it.** The reader it serves is the one who has been away for a while, and pruning it defeats the entire point. It costs one line per rename.

| You remember | It is now called | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Wide View | **Role Options** | The list of specific roles for the one direction you picked. The old screen showed all three directions at once; now you pick a direction and see its roles. |
| Deep Dive | **The Role** | The honest read on a single role. The old step compared three options side by side; now you open one role at a time and it opens with this. |
| The Lingo / Lingo & Playbook | **Industry Background** | The vocabulary, tools, and people who shape the space you are entering. Section 3 of the Focus Playbook. |
| Know Your Value | **Your Personal Brand** | The foundation Reimagine builds from your Orientation inputs. Same output, plainer name. |
| Explore Options | **Put It to Work** | The screen after your Personal Brand, where you choose between Career Paths and Add an Opportunity. |
| Two Doors / Choose Your Path | **Put It to Work** | The same screen. It went through two names before this one. |
| Pick a Direction | **Career Paths** | Where you choose among Familiar Ground, Industry Insider, and Work That Matters. |
| Upload a Live Opportunity | **Add an Opportunity** | Bring a job description in and get a playbook for that exact role. |
| Reimagine Help | **My Coach** | Your coach for the search. The button in the bottom-right corner of every screen, and its own sidebar entry. |

| The Complete screen | **Focus Playbooks** | The old screen was an end-of-journey destination; this is a library you come back to, and it is available the whole time. It was called My Playbooks until September 2026, by which point the opportunities it also held had moved to My Pipeline. |

| Phases | *retired* | Reimagine used to present itself as five numbered phases. It is a set of screens and playbooks you move through in the order that suits you, so the guide describes it that way. |

## Recent changes

Short notes on what moved recently and what it means for you. **These entries roll off after about 30 days.** The table above is what remains, so anything worth keeping permanently belongs there, not here.

**August 2026 — the guide matches the product again.** This guide was reorganized around the way Reimagine actually works today rather than the five-phase journey it used to describe. Three chapters became one (Your Focus Playbook), one became two (Put It to Work and Career Paths), and Focus Playbooks got the chapter it never had. If you ask My Coach about a feature, it is reading this version.

**August 2026 — Recruiters for This Path.** A bonus section in your Focus Playbook, under Go-to-Market. Direct outreach reaches the people who own the problem you solve; this finds the executive-search firms that fill roles like the one you are pursuing. It is opt-in and it starts short on purpose.

**August 2026 — Interview Team.** When you open an opportunity, you can now tell Reimagine who you expect to meet and what you know about them. It sharpens your Interview Prep and gives My Coach the context to help you prepare for specific people.

**August 2026 — the intake is about 30 minutes.** The guide used to give a shorter estimate in some places and a longer one in others. Thirty minutes is the honest number.

**August 2026 — Reimagine on a phone.** The navigation menu now tucks behind a menu button in the top-left corner on a narrow screen, so the content gets the full width instead of a sliver of it. Holding your phone upright works now; the old advice to turn it sideways is retired, along with the banner that used to say so.

**August 2026 — Resume Builder.** If your resume is out of date, thin, or missing, Reimagine now builds one with you rather than asking you to fix it first. Give it whatever you have, including just companies and titles, and you get a complete editable draft.
