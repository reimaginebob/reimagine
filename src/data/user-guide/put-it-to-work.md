# Put It to Work

Your Personal Brand is the foundation. This is the screen where you decide what to do with it.

Reimagine asks one question before anything else: do you want it to widen the field for you, or do you have a specific role in hand already? Both are real options. Neither is the "right" one, and you can do both.

## On the screen

The screen opens with the headline **Put It to Work** and a short framing: *Your foundation is set. Pick how to put it to use. You can come back to this choice anytime from the sidebar.*

Below the framing line, two cards sit side by side. Each carries an icon, a title, a one-line value statement, a short body, a preview footer (what you will see and how long it takes), and a call-to-action button.

**Career Paths.** A route icon in gold. The body reads: *See role options across Familiar Ground (where your credibility extends), Industry Insider (where your sector knowledge translates), and Work That Matters (where your values point).* The preview footer notes that you will see three directions with role candidates and reasoning, and that it takes about 90 seconds to generate. The call to action is **Start Exploring →** (solid gold button). Choose this when you want Reimagine to widen the field for you. Career Paths covers what happens next.

**Add an Opportunity.** A target icon in blue. The body reads: *Paste a job description and get a focused playbook for that exact role: how you fit, how to position yourself, what the conversation looks like. Add as many opportunities as you want.* The preview footer notes that you will see a playbook built on demand for one specific job, about 30 seconds per section. The call to action is **Add an Opportunity →** (outlined blue button). Choose this when you already have a posting in hand and want Reimagine focused on it. Add an Opportunity covers the module in detail.

Above the two cards, a single guidance line reads: *Not sure which to pick first? Career Paths is a good place to start: it shows the range of where your background takes you. Come back to add an opportunity once you have a specific role in mind.*

## How to choose

If you are early and want to see what is out there, start with Career Paths. It shows you a wider field than the one or two next steps most people can already name, one direction at a time.

If a posting has already landed in front of you and you want to be sharp for it this week, start with Add an Opportunity. You do not have to explore directions first.

The two are not mutually exclusive, and neither one closes the other off.

## You can come back to this anytime

**Put It to Work** is a permanent entry in the sidebar, with **Career Paths** and **Add an Opportunity** nested under it. Whichever you pick now, the other is one click away later, from anywhere in the app.

Your foundation does not get rebuilt when you switch. Your Personal Brand stays constant across every direction and every opportunity you explore. Only the parts that depend on the specific role get written fresh.

If you are returning after a previous version of Reimagine, a short welcome-back note appears above the headline. Your inputs and your Personal Brand are exactly where you left them. Roles you explored before regenerate fresh from your latest inputs, so if you want to keep a prior playbook word for word, save its PDF first.
