# Glossary

These are the terms you will encounter inside Reimagine, in this guide, or in the methodology behind the tool.

If a term you remember is not here, check **What's Changed**. It carries former names next to their current ones.

---

**Add an Opportunity.** The second of the two cards on Put It to Work, and a permanent sidebar entry under it. Paste or upload a job description and Reimagine builds an Opportunity Playbook for that exact role.

**Affintus.** A free 15-minute professional assessment Reimagine recommends if you do not already have one. It produces results you can paste into the Assessments input screen.

**Bridge Story.** Your answer to "tell me about yourself": a single flowing answer in your voice, calibrated to the direction you are exploring. It opens with the strongest human anchor in your inputs, connects that to one accomplishment that carries the theme, and closes on why your next move is the natural next step. When your profile holds a second strong opening, Reimagine adds a short coaching note below it. It is section 2 of the Focus Playbook, and it feeds every later step that needs a "tell me about yourself." Your Focus Playbook covers the model and how to use it.

**Career Club.** The company that builds Reimagine. Founded by Bob Goodwin.

**Career Paths.** The first of the two cards on Put It to Work, and a sidebar entry under it. It leads to the three directions, and from there to Role Options.

**Compare offers.** A button that appears in My Playbooks once you have logged an offer on two or more opportunities. It lines the offers up around the cash and benefits you can actually bank on, holds equity at $0 with its stage noted, and never ranks them. My Playbooks covers how to read it.

**Convictions, Clarity, Confidence, Contagious (the 4 C's).** The internal arc of the Reimagine experience. Convictions are what is true about you. Clarity follows from convictions and lets you see options clearly. Confidence comes from evidence. Contagious is the natural result. When you believe, others do too. The framework is part of the methodology, not exposed in the tool's outputs.

**Cover Letter.** A section of the Opportunity Playbook. A short draft letter tuned to the posting, in your voice, built as the sibling of your Go-to-Market outreach. It addresses the most relevant leader by first name where the About This Company research names one, and the team by function otherwise.

**Demo mode.** A guided walkthrough of the tool using a fictional executive, Sarah Chen. Reaching `?demo=true` runs the tour. The entry link is hidden from the interface while the tour is brought back in line with the current flow, so some of the steps it walks through carry older screen names.

**Dimensional read (how Reimagine reads career fit).** When Reimagine writes your Personal Brand, it assesses your fit across six dimensions internally and emits a reading on each. The read does not name these dimensions as labels (that is internal scaffolding), but if you are curious how the analysis is structured, here it is:

- **Function.** Your core skill or expertise. What you do for a living.
- **Industry.** The sector you work in or know well: healthcare, tech, finance, nonprofit, and so on. Industries have their own language, problems, and operating rhythms.
- **Position in the value chain.** Where you sit relative to the customer or end user: inside the operating company, on the vendor or supplier side, in consulting or advisory work, on the regulator side. The same industry looks different from different positions.
- **Scale.** The size of the organization or the P&L you have managed. Scale changes the day-to-day experience of the work.
- **Pace.** How fast the work moves: startup chaos, steady enterprise rhythm, regulated-industry cycles. People differ in which pace they thrive in.
- **Mission.** What the organization exists to do in the world: profit maximization, social impact, public health, education, innovation. Mission is the "why" that energizes or drains people over time.

Your Personal Brand names what is settled about your fit and what is open. Settled means your track record points clearly; open means the next move has real choices. The dimensions are how Reimagine thinks; the settled and open framing is what reaches the page.

**Direction.** One of the three choices on Career Paths: Familiar Ground, Industry Insider, or Work That Matters. You pick one at a time, and Reimagine generates role options for that direction only. You can explore all three, and each one keeps its own options.

**Familiar Ground.** One of the three directions. Same kind of work in a new seat: a bigger scope, the same role elsewhere, or your function in a new industry. Where your existing track record speaks most directly.

**Focus Playbook.** The single assembled page Reimagine builds for one role you opened from Role Options. It opens with an orientation card ("How to use your playbook") and holds seven numbered sections grouped into four named groups, plus Recruiters for This Path and Income Now as unnumbered bonuses. Group 1, Understand the role: The Role. Group 2, Build your story: Your Bridge Story, Industry Background. Group 3, Prepare for the conversation: Interview Prep (with STAR stories inside it). Group 4, Carry it into the market: Resume Refresh, LinkedIn Remix, Go-to-Market. The Role generates immediately; the rest generate on demand.

**Go-to-Market.** Section 7 of the Focus Playbook, in Group 4. A target company list, a hiring executive profile, a personalized outreach template, and a LinkedIn signal tweak, built around the role you are exploring. It runs live web research, so it is the most expensive section to generate and sits late in the arc on purpose.

**Golden Thread.** The single consistent theme that runs through your accomplishments, your wiring, and your reputation. Surfaced as the lead line of your Personal Brand.

**Ikigai.** The Japanese concept used in the *Work That Matters* direction. The intersection of what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for.

**Income Now.** A bonus section at the bottom of the Focus Playbook, not numbered alongside the seven, and also its own sidebar entry. A consulting and fractional income plan for generating revenue while you search. Optional; skip it if income continuity is not a concern for you right now.

**Industry Background.** Section 3 of the Focus Playbook, in the Build your story group. The vocabulary, the tools, and the people who shape the space you are entering.

**Industry Insider.** One of the three directions. The wider set of organizations around your industry: clients, vendors, consultants, regulators, adjacent players. Where your insider knowledge is a competitive advantage.

**Interview Prep.** Section 4 of the Focus Playbook, and a section of the Opportunity Playbook. The questions this role's interview cycle is most likely to surface, with framing recommendations and STAR stories placed under the questions they best answer.

**Interview Team.** A section of the Opportunity Playbook, and the one you fill in rather than generate. How you came across the role, what you already know about it, and each person you expect to meet. Your answers sharpen Interview Prep and give My Coach context. The notes stay private to your account.

**KEEL principles.** The foundation of the *Making Your Own Weather* methodology. Mentioned in marketing materials but not exposed inside the tool's outputs.

**Key Accomplishments.** The section of your resume that holds your strongest career achievements, reframed as money made, money saved, or risk mitigated with numbers attached. The Resume Refresh section of your Focus Playbook generates these for you. In *Making Your Own Weather*, this same idea is called your "Greatest Hits"; Reimagine uses Key Accomplishments as the user-facing label because it reads cleanly as a resume section header. Career Highlights, Career Milestones, or Selected Achievements work equally well if you prefer one of those.

**LinkedIn Remix.** Section 6 of the Focus Playbook. Recommended headline, About section, and experience copy for the role you are pursuing. Reimagine writes the copy; you apply it to your profile yourself.

**Local storage.** A fallback Reimagine uses to hold your work in the browser when you have declined to sign in. Signed in, your work lives in Career Club's database keyed to your email and follows you to any device you sign in from. Saving and Sharing Your Work covers both.

**Made money / Saved money / Mitigated risk.** The three business imperatives Reimagine uses to translate your accomplishments. Every accomplishment in your output should be framed as one of these three with a number attached.

**Making Your Own Weather (MYOW).** The book by Bob Goodwin that the Reimagine methodology is built on.

**My Coach.** Your coach for the search, grounded in *Making Your Own Weather* and in what Reimagine knows about you. It is a sidebar entry and a button in the bottom-right corner of every other screen. Everything it knows about you came from you; it never looks you up.

**My Playbooks.** The sidebar entry listing every Focus Playbook and Opportunity Playbook you have built, split into those two groups. Open any of them to pick up where you left off. This is where your work lives.

**Opportunity Playbook.** The playbook Reimagine builds from a job description you bring in through Add an Opportunity. Its rail lists About This Company, Compensation, Where you fit, Resume Refresh, Cover Letter, Interview Team, Interview Prep, and Offer & Negotiation.

**Orientation.** The intake, and the only part of the tool where you do the writing. Ten screens covering location, resume, LinkedIn, assessment, values, practical priorities, reputation, your story, and your skills.

**Personal Brand.** The foundation Reimagine builds from everything you provided in Orientation, and the one output that stays constant across every direction and role you explore. It is a structured read rather than a block of prose: a through-line at the top, a proof strip when your numbers support one, labelled sections that appear only when your inputs earn them, an origin passage when your story inputs hold a real one, growth edges when there are real ones, and a close that opens up what is next. Behind the scenes it is a two-stage build; on the screen it is one read.

**Practical Priorities.** The Orientation screen covering what a move actually needs to be worth making: your compensation floor, commute or remote needs, how much benefits weigh, your stability-versus-upside lean, and any hard deal-breakers. The screen itself is headed "Your Priorities & Non-Negotiables". Your deal-breakers and your stability lean shape which companies Go-to-Market surfaces; the compensation floor and commute needs are held back for when you evaluate an actual offer.

**Put It to Work.** The screen you reach after your Personal Brand, and a permanent sidebar entry. Two cards: **Career Paths** leads to picking a direction and exploring roles; **Add an Opportunity** takes a pasted job description and builds a playbook for that exact role. Both stay available; picking one never closes the other.

**Recruiters for This Path.** An unnumbered bonus section of the Focus Playbook, below Go-to-Market. A short, live-researched list of executive-search firms that specialize in your function, industry, and level, in two kinds: boutique or independent firms, and practices inside large firms. Names are shown only when they trace to a checkable first-party source.

**Refine box.** The "Does this feel right?" box below every generated output. Where you tell Reimagine what to adjust before regenerating.

**Reputation.** An Orientation screen. External data about how others see you, gathered through four short prompts.

**Resume Builder.** The path on the Resume screen for anyone whose resume is out of date, thin, or missing. Give it an old resume, a LinkedIn PDF, or just companies and titles, and it writes a complete draft you edit on the page. It has its own sidebar entry and your draft is saved, so you can come back and add to it.

**Resume Refresh.** Section 5 of the Focus Playbook, and a section of the Opportunity Playbook. Your resume rewritten so the strongest evidence for the role you are exploring is visible in the first seven seconds, downloadable as a Word document in a Human version and an ATS version.

**Role Options.** The screen that lists role options for the one direction you picked. An umbrella paragraph carries the credibility case for the direction, then each option shows a title, an organization type, a vehicle, and a sector. Click any one to open its full Focus Playbook. It also holds the "Tell us where else to look" box and honest handling when only a few strong options exist.

**Sarah Chen.** The fictional executive used in Reimagine's demo mode. A VP of Talent Acquisition in healthcare with 15 years of experience.

**Save Playbook as PDF.** The button in the playbook footer that assembles every generated section for the current role into one printable document. Useful for sharing with a coach or mentor, reading on a device where you are not signed in, or keeping an annotated reference alongside your offline notes.

**STAR (Situation, Thinking, Action, Result).** The interview story framework Reimagine uses inside Interview Prep, where each story sits next to the question it best answers. The T stands for *Thinking*, not Tasks, because the most important part of any interview story is how you thought through the problem.

**The Role.** Section 1 of the Focus Playbook, and the one that generates immediately. An honest read on what the role is, why you fit, and what to weigh. The Opportunity Playbook's equivalent is called **Where you fit**.

**TMAY.** "Tell me about yourself." The interview question the Bridge Story is built to answer, as a single answer in your voice you can say in about 30 seconds.

**Where you fit.** The Opportunity Playbook's read on a specific posting: how you align, where the role stretches you, and the watch-outs. It finishes with a 30-second bridge story tuned to that posting. Same job as **The Role** in a Focus Playbook, named for the fact that it reads against one job rather than a whole direction.

**Work That Matters.** One of the three directions. The Ikigai direction, for people ready for more meaning in their work or at a stage where legacy matters more than maximizing comp.

**Your Skills.** The final Orientation screen. Reimagine reads your resume and LinkedIn and extracts a categorized list of your hard skills as editable chips: technical tools, systems and platforms, certifications, languages, and methodologies. The list sharpens which options fit you, which companies match, and what the resume and LinkedIn copy lead with.
