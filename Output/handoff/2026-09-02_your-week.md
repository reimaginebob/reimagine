## Pre-flight discovery (scope correction)

Drafted against `main` at `1b5ae00`. What this found:

- **The read side is already complete.** `buildPursuitStatusBlock` in `api/coach.js` turns a user's pursuit rows into a per-opportunity digest — stage, next meeting, next step with an `OVERDUE by N days` count, `no status change in N days`, days in pipeline, the connector's situation note, and whether Interview Prep is built — plus a rollup (`4 active, 1 needing attention, 2 going quiet`). A weekly plan needs exactly this and nothing more. **The hard part is written.**
- **It is not reachable from the client.** It is a module-private function inside a serverless handler. Change 1 below extracts it rather than reimplementing it, because it carries non-obvious correctness that a second implementation would lose: the wrong-year guard (`insane()`), the `Date`-to-ISO normalisation that fixed dates being read as 9,131 days overdue, and the rule that a past meeting means "last met", never "overdue".
- **Generation infrastructure is standard.** `generateSection(id, promptFn, opts)` + `outputs.<id>` + `sectionErrors` + `Loading` + `OutPanel` is the shipped pattern; `income` is the closest standalone example (`src/App.jsx:13537-13553`).
- **Printing already works.** `src/print.css` is a paper stylesheet. The "artifact" Bob wants is a Print / Save as PDF affordance on a screen that renders from live data, not a generated document that goes stale.
- **A weekly plan does NOT need a new table.** The moves it proposes are next steps on opportunities, and `pursuit_status.next_move` / `next_step_at` already hold those. Tapping a proposed move writes it there, which means next week's plan reads its own output and "did it happen" is already visible as overdue-or-cleared. A separate commitments table was considered and rejected for v1 on that basis.
- **What genuinely has no home:** networking outreach, recruiter relationships, and applications with no saved opportunity. Nothing in the schema models them. That is a real gap and it is deferred, not solved — see Out of scope.
- **The user guide has a trap.** `src/data/user-guide/ORDER.json` warns that any listed chapter is interpolated into the Coach's cached system block **for every user on every turn**. A gated pilot must therefore stay out of ORDER.json and out of `FEATURE_MAP`, exactly as Your STAR Stories did while it was internal-only.

## Specific changes

### 1. `src/pipeline-digest.js` — extract, do not reimplement

Create the file. Move `buildPursuitStatusBlock` and its helpers out of `api/coach.js` **unchanged in behaviour**, splitting it into two exports so the client can render structure and the server can keep its prose block:

- `buildPipelineRows(savedRecords, pursuitRows, now)` → the per-opportunity array plus the rollup counts (`active`, `attention`, `quiet`). Pure; takes `now` so it is testable.
- `buildPursuitStatusBlock(state, pursuitRows)` → unchanged signature and unchanged output string, now built on top of `buildPipelineRows`.

**Byte-level requirement:** the string `api/coach.js` sends must be identical to what it sends today for the same inputs. Verify by capturing the current output for a representative row set before the change and diffing after. If it differs at all, the extraction is wrong.

**Extension `.js`, not `.mjs`, and no JSX** — this file is imported across the `api/*` ↔ `src/*` boundary. CLAUDE.md §8 documents the 2026-05-27 `FUNCTION_INVOCATION_FAILED` outage (PR #76, reverted at `940557b`) caused by a `.mjs` import across exactly this boundary. `.js` is the proven-safe form (`api/chat.js` → `src/data/user-guide-content.js`). Follow `src/nav-labels.js`'s header comment as the model for saying so in the file.

**5c. The prompt builder.** Add `week` to the `P` object. It receives the digest rows from change 1, not raw pursuit rows, so the day counts the model states are computed rather than inferred.

Required content of the prompt:

- **Ground every move in a named opportunity and a named fact from the digest.** A move must trace to something in the rows — an overdue step, a stage with no meeting booked, an opportunity gone quiet for N days, prep not built before an interview. This is the Coach's GROUND BEFORE YOU ASSERT rule applied to a surface that would otherwise be very easy to fill with plausible advice.
- **Three to five moves. Never more.** A weekly plan with nine items is a list, and a list is what the person already has.
- **Each move: a short imperative headline, one or two lines of why it matters this week, and — where the digest supports it — the opportunity it belongs to.** Visual hierarchy per CLAUDE.md §3: the headline carries the insight, the prose supports it.
- **A thin pipeline is a prospecting week, not an apology.** Fewer than two active opportunities: the plan is direct outreach and target research, grounded in the RFP framing and the three reasons a company worth writing to has nothing posted (already in the Coach's knowledge, `api/coach.js` — reuse the substance, do not re-author it). Never open by telling someone their pipeline is thin.
- **At most two questions at the end**, and only about what the data cannot show — outreach they have sent, recruiter conversations, applications with no saved opportunity. Never questions the digest already answers.
- **Refuse to invent activity.** Explicitly: the plan may not assert that an employer went silent, that a follow-up was sent, or that anything happened outside the rows and notes provided. Where a situation note exists, it may be relayed as reported fact and not extended.
- The full standard voice-rule stack every Clarity-producing prompt carries (CLAUDE.md §5): EVIDENCE-BASED CONFIDENCE, EVIDENCE-ANCHORED PATTERNS, NO TYPOLOGY LABELS, NO AI-COACHING REGISTER, EPISTEMIC CALIBRATION, REFUSE overclaim, TRANSLATION NOT PRAISE, LOGIC-FLIP CADENCE refusal.

**5d. The render case.** Add `case'week':` beside `case'pipeline':` (line 13503). It renders:

- An `<h1>` of `NAV_LABELS.week` and a one-line subtitle.
- A `CoachingCallout` (the gold accented block, `src/App.jsx:5872`) explaining what the screen is — required by CLAUDE.md §8's instructions-get-a-distinct-treatment rule; do not style this as a gray paragraph.
- A pipeline-at-a-glance line built from the digest rollup, rendered from live data on every render — **not** from the generated text. This is the part that must never be stale.
- The generated plan via the standard `Loading` / `sectionErrors` / `OutPanel` trio, following the `income` case (13537-13553) as the pattern, with a `Generate` button when `!outputs.week` and a `Regenerate` when it exists.
- A **Print / Save as PDF** action.
- A "last built" timestamp beside the plan, and — when any pursuit row has been updated since the plan was generated — a plain line saying the pipeline has moved since this was built, with the Regenerate action next to it. This is the honesty mechanism for the one genuinely stale part of the screen, and it is not optional.

**5e. Reachability.** `week` must round-trip through whatever step-name lookup and navigation guards the app already runs (`nav('week')`, back/forward, the section-name lookup). Verify a flagged user can reach it and a non-flagged user cannot reach it by URL either — a step that renders for someone with no rail entry is a dead screen with a live route.

## Out of scope

- **A commitments table.** v1's moves are next steps on opportunities, which the schema already holds.
- **Non-opportunity activity** — networking outreach, recruiter relationships, applications with no saved opportunity. This is the real modelling gap and it needs its own brief. v1 asks about it in prose and carries the answer for that session only; it does not pretend to track it.
- **The Friday or Monday review loop** ("you committed to four things, three happened"). It is the highest-value half of the idea and it needs a week of real plans to exist before it can be designed against anything real.
- **The Monday email.** `campaign_sends` already enforces one campaign email per person per week and the Resend rail is live, so this is reachable — but sending is the one action that cannot be undone, and it must not ride along with a pilot.
- **The Claude artifact version** of Your Week (live via the `Career Club Reimagine` connector's `list_pursuits`, writing back through `update_pursuit`). That is a prototype outside this repo, useful for judging whether a weekly plan is worth looking at before this screen is refined. It is not a repo change and is not gated by these flags.
- **Any change to `api/pursuit-status.js` or a migration.** Neither is needed.
- User guide and `FEATURE_MAP` wiring — see the GA checklist.

Your Week: a five-day plan built from the pipeline

My Pipeline answers "where does everything stand". Nobody opens a job search
tool wanting that answer -- they want to know what to do next. Your Week asks
the same rows a different question and proposes three to five moves, each one
traceable to a fact already in the pipeline: an overdue step, a stage with no
meeting booked, an opportunity quiet for three weeks, prep not built before
an interview. A thin pipeline produces a prospecting week, not an apology.

Coach reads to say "overdue by twelve days" -- but it lived module-private
inside api/coach.js. It moves to src/pipeline-digest.js, imported by both, so
the screen and the Coach cannot disagree about what is overdue. The
extraction is behaviour-preserving; the string coach.js sends is byte-
identical for the same inputs. Plain .js across the api/src boundary, never
.mjs (PR #76).

No new table. A proposed move is a next step on an opportunity, which
pursuit_status has held since August -- so next week's plan reads its own
output and whether something happened is already visible. The page also
renders its at-a-glance line from live rows rather than from generated text,
and says plainly when the pipeline has moved since the plan was built. That
is the point of it: a weekly plan someone wants to look at is the reason to
keep the dates underneath it current.

Behind a per-user flag.
