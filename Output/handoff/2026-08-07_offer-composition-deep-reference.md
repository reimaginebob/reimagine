# Part 1: The Taxonomy

## A. Cash Compensation
- **Base salary.** Annualized (exempt) / hourly (non-exempt). Annual merit cycle; true COLA rare in US private sector. Geographic pay differentials material (GitLab: SF benchmark × Location Factor × Level Factor; Google/Meta metro zones). Ask which pay zone applies, whether locked at hire, what happens on relocation. ~10 states + several cities require posted ranges as of 2026.
- **Sign-on bonuses.** Flat one-time, increasingly split. Almost always clawback (full/prorated if you leave within a window, commonly 12mo). Taxed as supplemental wages, 22% withholding up to $1M. Ask exact clawback window/proration, whether layoff excuses repayment, and whether repayment is gross or net of tax withheld.
- **Annual/performance bonuses.** Guaranteed / target / discretionary. Payout = base × target% × company multiplier × individual multiplier, prorated for partial year. "Active employment on payout date" clause can forfeit an earned bonus. Ask target %, y1 guaranteed?, historical company-multiplier range, exact payout date + forfeiture condition.
- **Spot/retention bonuses.** Spot small/ad hoc. Retention larger, lump or vesting tranches, clawed back if you leave before vesting.
- **Commission plans.** OTE = base + variable at 100% (a target). 2026 mix: SDR/BDR ~70-75% base; AE ~50-60%; Field/Enterprise ~50/50. Scrutinize quota methodology, accelerators, draws (recoverable vs non-recoverable), churn clawbacks (90d-12mo), ramp (3-6mo). Plans typically at-will, revisable mid-year.
- **Other cash.** Relocation (fully taxable since 2018; check gross-up + repayment), referral, milestone (get sign-off criteria in writing), cash profit-sharing.
- **Cross-cutting question:** what event triggers forfeiture, and what is the exact date/condition for payment. The gap between "earned" and "paid" is where disputes originate.

## B. Equity Compensation
- **RSUs.** 4-year vest / 1-year cliff, refreshes from y2-3. Taxed ordinary income at vest on FMV-at-vest (sell-to-cover). Public = single-trigger; private = double-trigger (time + liquidity event). Unvested almost always forfeited on termination. Ask single/double-trigger, refresh cadence, layoff treatment.
- **Options ISO vs NSO.** NSO: ordinary income on spread at exercise. ISO: no regular tax at exercise but spread is AMT preference item; qualifying disposition (2yr grant / 1yr exercise) = LTCG. $100K ISO limit (§422(d)); excess auto-NSO. Private strike ≥ 409A FMV. Post-termination exercise historically 90 days (~10% of startups extend, up to ~20% in layoff periods). Early exercise + 83(b) within 30 days can start clocks early, risks cash loss. Red flag: 90-day window, no early exercise, private co, no exit timeline.
- **ESPP.** Qualified 423: ~15% discount, often lookback. IRS caps $25,000 grant-date FMV/yr. Close to free money.
- **Restricted stock + 83(b).** True restricted stock at very early startups; 83(b) within 30 days pays ordinary income on near-zero grant value, starts LTCG clock; non-refundable risk.
- **Phantom/SARs/profit interests.** Phantom/SARs = cash promises, taxed as bonus. LLC profit interests (PE portfolios) = appreciation above a hurdle; under Rev Proc 93-27 can be tax-free at grant with later LTCG. Ask: real equity, profits interest, or phantom/SAR, and the hurdle.
- **Private valuation risk.** 409A conservative, goes stale. Liquidation preference stack: preferred paid before common; headline exit can leave common ~worthless. Discount illiquid private equity heavily vs public RSUs. Ask total raised + preference terms (1x non-participating vs participating), preferred stack vs last valuation, gap between last 409A and last preferred round. Red flag: refusal to disclose preference stack / dilution.
- **Change of control.** Assumed / cashed out / accelerated. Single-trigger = vests on close; double-trigger = close + termination without cause within window. Ask single/double-trigger + assumed-or-cashed-out.

## C. Benefits and Insurance (2026 IRS figures)
- **Health.** HMO/PPO/EPO/HDHP. Need four numbers: premium + employer/employee split by tier, deductible, OOP max (2026 ACA cap $10,600 self / $21,200 family), copay/coinsurance. Request the SBC per employer.
- **HSA/FSA.** HSA needs HDHP, triple tax-advantaged, portable, rolls over. FSA any plan, use-it-or-lose-it. 2026: HSA $4,400 self / $8,750 family (55+ catch-up $1,000); HDHP min deductible $1,700/$3,400; Healthcare FSA $3,400 ($680 carryover); Dependent Care FSA $7,500 (up from $5,000, OBBBA). Employers often seed HSA $500-$1,000 self / $1,000-$2,000 family.
- **Dental/vision.** Dental 100/80/50, annual max $1,000-2,000, ortho often excluded/adult-excluded. Vision: exam + modest allowance.
- **401(k).** 2026: deferral $24,500; 50+ catch-up $8,000; 60-63 super catch-up $11,250; 415(c) total $72,000 ($81,250 w/ catch-up); comp limit $360,000. Common match 100% up to 3% or 50% up to 6% (both = 3% employer max). Match vesting: 3-yr cliff or 2-6yr graded; Safe Harbor immediate. Mega backdoor Roth rare/high-value. Pensions rare outside gov/union/academia/utilities.
- **Life/disability.** Basic life ~1x salary. STD 60-70% for weeks-6mo after 0-14d elimination. LTD 50-66% after 90-180d, taxability depends who pays premium. Disability underrated by candidates.
- **PTO/leave.** Unlimited PTO: ~16 days taken vs ~14 accrued (only 2-day diff), forfeits cash-out value; only ~7% of employers. Ask what departing employees actually take + payout at termination. Parental leave big tech/pro-services ~14-18wk median primary; overall US ~8wk; no federal mandate. Ask primary/secondary + 100% salary?
- **Other perks.** Tuition ~$5,250/yr (§127 threshold). Student loan repayment. Wellness/remote stipends $50-150/mo (taxable). Commuter 2026 $340/mo transit + parking. Relocation fully taxable, ask gross-up. Visa sponsorship ($10-25K+ employer cost) make-or-break. Dependent care FSA, pet insurance, legal plans modest.

## D. Legal and Risk Terms
- **At-will.** Standard (Montana exception). Don't spend negotiating capital here.
- **Non-competes.** FTC 2024 ban enjoined + withdrawn by Feb 2026; no federal ban, state law governs. Banned: CA, MN, ND, OK, DC (MT outside business-sale). Heavily restricted: CO, IL, WA, OR, NH, VT, MA, UT (often salary thresholds). Scrutinize duration (6-12mo common, 2yr+ aggressive), geography ("worldwide" red flag for remote), "competitor" definition. Often negotiable for ICs; new consideration often required if asked to sign after starting.
- **Non-solicit/non-disparagement.** Survive even in ban states. Non-solicit 12-24mo, watch overbroad scope. Non-disparagement should carve out gov agencies (EEOC/NLRB/SEC).
- **IP/invention assignment.** Assigns work "during employment"/"using resources." Fill in the Prior Inventions Schedule. Many states (CA Labor Code §2870) require a personal-time carve-out; confirm the notice is present.
- **Arbitration/class waivers.** ~55-60%+ covered, near-universal at large employers. EFASASHA (Mar 2022) invalidates pre-dispute arbitration for sexual assault/harassment. Usually non-negotiable; understand what's given up.
- **Clawbacks.** Sign-on repayment most common. Commission clawbacks on churn. CA AB 692 ("stay-or-pay," eff Jan 1 2026) restricts these for CA employees. Dodd-Frank/SEC 10D-1 exec clawback (since Dec 2023).
- **Severance/CoC/notice.** Most non-exec offers say nothing (at-will = none owed). When present, ~2-4wk IC, more for managers, contingent on release. Golden parachutes exec/VP+. WARN Act: 100+ employee cos, 60d notice before mass layoff (state mini-WARNs stricter).
- **Contingencies.** Background/reference/drug/I-9/E-Verify/credential checks, resolve after acceptance. If a contingency fails, offer can be rescinded, no recourse under at-will. **Don't resign a current job until contingencies formally clear.**

# Part 2: Valuation Methodology

## The additive formula
**Total Comp = Base + Guaranteed Cash + Risk-Adjusted Bonus/Commission EV + Equity EV + Monetized Benefits**
- Base: fully guaranteed, use as-is.
- Guaranteed cash (sign-on, relocation): model as "guaranteed only if tenure clears the clawback window."
- Bonus/commission EV: never the headline target. Multiply target by realistic attainment (avg ~43%; SDR ~53%; enterprise AE ~38%; only ~28% expect team to hit 100%), prorate partial year by months/12. Best source: ask hiring manager for team historical attainment distribution.

## Equity expected value
- **Public RSUs:** EV = unvested shares × current price, by tranche for annualized. Offer a volatility haircut / low-base-high sensitivity.
- **Private:** face value misleads. Apply DLOM 20-40% by time-to-liquidity. Formal: OPM (common = call option struck at preference threshold) and PWERM (probability-weighted exit scenarios). Practical scenario table ≈ 40% total loss / 35% modest / 20% moderate (2-5x) / 5% outsized (10x+), summed prob × outcome. Stage drives discount. Default: Private Equity EV = Grant Value (at 409A) × Stage-Based Discount Factor — **Series A/B ~0.15-0.30, Series C/D ~0.35-0.55, late-stage/pre-IPO ~0.60-0.80**, adjustable not fixed.

## Benefits monetization
- **401(k) match** = match % × min(salary, match cap). Direct cash equivalent.
- **Health value** = employer premium contribution − what employee would otherwise pay for equivalent coverage (COBRA/marketplace benchmark). NOT gross premium.
- **PTO** = (salary / working days per year) × PTO days, ~260 working days denominator.
- **Flexibility/remote** = NBER-backed; 5-8% of salary hybrid, up to 25% full remote. Calculator default: user-adjustable slider ~5-10% of base.

## Multi-offer comparison logic (four steps)
1. Compute each offer's risk-adjusted Total Comp EV (formula above).
2. **Cost-of-living normalize the CASH components only** (never equity — not location-dependent) via COL index ratio.
3. Apply a **user-specified risk-tolerance multiplier to equity EV specifically** (risk-averse + limited savings → discount private/early equity further).
4. **Present a conservative case AND an optimistic case side by side**, not a single blended number — false precision is less useful and less honest than an explicit range.

# Part 3: What This Means for the Reimagine Feature

- **Data model:** `offerStage` needs base, sign-on (+clawback window), target bonus % (+guaranteed/payout date/forfeiture), commission basics (OTE split, draw type, quota, clawback), equity type (RSU/ISO/NSO/other) + grant size + vesting + **company stage (public/late-private/early-private)** for discounting, and benefits actually disclosed (premium split, deductible, 401k formula+vesting). Some fields inferable/defaulted; schema needs room for all.
- **Calculator logic:** additive Total Comp EV; stage-based equity discount (adjustable slider); benefits monetization (401k, health subsidy, PTO); conservative/optimistic dual-case, not one number. Net-new prompt-and-calculation work, downstream of the compensation read.
- **Risk-flag checklist (static content):** forfeiture triggers + payout dates on every cash element; single/double-trigger vesting; exercise-window length; preference-stack disclosure; non-compete scope; Prior Inventions Schedule; contingency-timing warning. This is the "total comp checklist" + "script library" substance.
- **Coach handoff (judgment calls):** how much to discount a specific private company's equity; whether to push on a non-compete given state law + next-job plans; whether visa value outweighs lower base; how to weigh risky-high-equity vs stable-high-base given the person's savings cushion. Guide chapter draws on Part 1 facts as ground truth, Coach reasons case-by-case.
